Basic Care for All Birds

  • Cage: large enough for wing stretching and short flights; away from drafts, kitchens, and smoke.
  • Hygiene: clean the cage floor daily, wash food and water dishes daily, disinfect weekly.
  • Water: fresh drinking water daily; bathing water 2–3 times weekly.
  • Sleep: 10–12 hours of dark, quiet sleep.
  • Temperature: avoid extreme heat, cold drafts, and sudden changes.
  • Interaction: parrots and social birds need daily attention, toys, and enrichment.
  • Air safety: avoid overheated non-stick cookware, perfume sprays, aerosols, smoke, and strong cleaning chemicals.

Health & Nutrition

🥚 Proteins and Supplements

  • Protein: boiled egg, mealworms or crickets for insect-eaters, cooked legumes, and sprouted seeds.
  • Calcium: cuttlebone and mineral blocks, especially for laying females.
  • Vitamins: vitamin A from carrots and leafy greens; vitamin D3 from safe sunlight; supplements only with veterinary advice.
  • Molting: provide extra protein and amino acids to support feather regrowth.

💊 Common Illnesses

  • Diarrhea and infections: treatment depends on the cause and may require a veterinarian.
  • Parasites: dewormers and doses must be prescribed by an avian vet.
  • Mites and lice: use bird-safe treatment and deep-clean the cage.
  • Fungal disease: requires veterinary diagnosis and prescription treatment.
🩺 Important: This website gives general educational information only. Never give antibiotics, dewormers, or human medicines without an avian veterinarian. Seek urgent help for fluffed feathers, lethargy, appetite loss, breathing difficulty, or abnormal droppings.

⚠️ Foods and Habits to Avoid

Never feed birds: avocado, chocolate, caffeine, alcohol, salty foods, large amounts of onion or garlic, apple seeds, fruit pits, moldy food, or unknown wild plants. Avocado contains persin, while chocolate and caffeine can seriously affect the nervous system and heart.
