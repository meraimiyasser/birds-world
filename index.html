Basic Care for All Birds

  • Cage: large enough for wing stretching and short flights; away from drafts, kitchens, and smoke.
  • Hygiene: clean the cage floor daily and wash food and water dishes daily.
  • Water: fresh drinking water daily; bathing water 2–3 times weekly.
  • Sleep: 10–12 hours of dark, quiet sleep.
  • Temperature: avoid extreme heat, cold drafts, and sudden changes.
  • Safety: avoid overheated non-stick cookware, perfumes, aerosols, smoke, and strong cleaning fumes.

Health & Nutrition

  • Protein: boiled egg, mealworms for insect-eating birds, cooked legumes, and sprouted seeds.
  • Calcium: cuttlebone and mineral blocks, especially for laying females.
  • Vitamins: vitamin A from carrots and leafy greens; vitamin D3 from safe sunlight.
  • Molting: provide extra protein and calcium to support feather regrowth.
  • Illness: never give medicines without an avian veterinarian’s advice.
🩺 Important: Seek urgent veterinary help for fluffed feathers, lethargy, loss of appetite, breathing difficulty, or abnormal droppings.

⚠️ Foods to Avoid

Never feed birds: avocado, chocolate, caffeine, alcohol, salty foods, onion, garlic, apple seeds, fruit pits, or moldy food.
